# 🍌 Bananas Are Slightly Radioactive

> Category: Fun Fact · 2026-09-25

Bananas contain potassium-40, a naturally radioactive isotope. The radiation is so tiny that scientists invented the "banana equivalent dose" as an informal unit.

You'd need to eat about 10 million bananas at once for it to be lethal — at which point radiation would be the least of your problems.
